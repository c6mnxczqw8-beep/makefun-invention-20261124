# Candidate pitches (draft round)

Twelve one-sentence candidates, drafted before picking the winner.
The comedy brief: a useless / absurd product pitched with total
startup sincerity, fixing a tiny everyday annoyance.

1. A snooze button for other people's alarms, so you can silence your
   roommate's 6 a.m. wake-up from your own bed without getting up.
2. An umbrella for your phone's feelings, which opens whenever a harsh
   text arrives so the phone only has to read it in the shade.
3. Tiny windshield wipers for your wrists that clear every splash
   before the wet-sleeve trickle reaches your elbow while you wash
   your face.
4. A doorframe that notices when you forget why you walked into a
   room, pushes you back out, and replays what you were thinking so
   you can enter again properly.  <- WINNER (see notes)
5. A USB plug that apologises, rotates itself, and only goes in on
   the third try so it still feels like a normal USB plug.
6. Couples counselling for socks, held inside the washing machine,
   so no sock ever leaves the marriage and vanishes alone again.
7. A fridge light with a tiny curtain, so your midnight snack stays
   private and the fridge stops reporting you to the whole kitchen.
8. An elevator button that pretends you pressed it first, closing
   the doors a second slower for anyone running and shouting "hold it".
9. A shopping-cart wheel that goes to therapy for its wobble and
   comes back rolling straight, confident, and no longer clicking.
10. Headphones that untangle themselves overnight out of guilt,
    leaving a small note saying it will not happen again.
11. A kettle that files for overtime whenever you boil it twice in
    an hour, then sighs and makes the tea anyway.
12. Sticker-peel gloves that remove price-sticker residue in one
    pull and frame the clean surface as a tiny trophy.

## Why #4 won

- The annoyance (walking into a room and instantly forgetting why)
  is universal, tiny, and instantly recognised.
- The fix is silly yet weirdly plausible: if forgetting happens at
  the doorway, a doorway that rewinds you is the logical absurd fix.
- The laugh is in the invention itself (being politely ejected and
  replayed your own plan), not in a joke bolted on at the end.
- It is tighter than the sock / USB ideas, which lean on familiar
  reheated premises, and more original than the alarm snooze button.

## Refinement (second pass)

Draft said "replays what you were thinking" — vague and a bit
mind-readery. Refined to replaying your own muttered plan (people
really do mutter "right, scissors..." on the way), and "the second
entrance actually works" gives the sentence a confident startup
payoff instead of the flatter "enter again properly".
